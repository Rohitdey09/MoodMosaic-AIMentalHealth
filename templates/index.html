Welcome to MoodMosiac

With busy lifestyles, people often struggle to focus on their mental well-being. Consulting a therapist each time they feel distressed can be challenging, leading many to rely on online resources for mental health advice. Unfortunately, these resources can sometimes mislead or overwhelm users, contributing to confusion or even worsening anxiety. Through machine learning, we can address this gap by developing a system that detects emotions and provides accurate mental health insights. This technology empowers users to better understand and manage their mental health in a safe and accessible way.


MoodMosiac

Purpose of the web-app

This project aims to design a web application that enables users to assess their mental health status by detecting emotions and offering personalized insights with a confidence score. It provides a more reliable alternative to random online searches, helping users avoid misinformation and promoting accurate self-reflection. This tool is particularly valuable for those with limited access to mental health resources, such as students in remote areas, offering them a convenient way to monitor their well-being and make informed decisions about seeking further support if needed.

Online resources for mental health advice can often be misleading, sometimes framing mild issues as severe conditions, which can induce unnecessary worry and stress. By using machine learning, we address this problem through a model trained to assess emotional states accurately based on user input. This allows us to offer more precise mental health insights, reducing the risk of misinformation and unnecessary anxiety, and providing a dependable resource for individuals seeking support for their well-being.
